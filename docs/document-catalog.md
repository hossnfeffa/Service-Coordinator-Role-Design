# Document Catalog

What each document is designed to do. Content, names, and environment specifics are intentionally omitted.

**Legend:** 🆕 Built from scratch · 🔄 Rewritten / modernized · 🔗 Consolidated from multiple sources

---

## 0. Foundation

### Core Service Desk SOP 🔄
**Purpose:** The central operating handbook for the service desk, covering how the team works day to day.
**Designed to:**
- Set ticket handling and quality expectations from intake to closure, with acceptable and unacceptable examples
- Define **KPIs by stakeholder group** (service desk, IT operations, executive leadership) and say whether a high or low value is better for each
- Outline weekday and weekend **on-call expectations**
- Explain how to request planned and unplanned **time off**
- Explain what each **team communication channel** is for, so questions and updates go to the right place

---

## 1. Incident Management

### Major Incident (P1) Management SOP 🔄
**Purpose:** Defines how the team recognizes, declares, coordinates, communicates, and closes out a critical, business-wide outage.
**Designed to:**
- Give a clear trigger for when an incident becomes a major incident
- Assign coordination roles so nobody is guessing who's in charge
- Set communication cadence for stakeholders during the incident
- Require post-incident review and root cause analysis

### Major Incident Quick Reference 🆕
**Purpose:** A one-page version of the full P1 SOP, built for use *during* an incident.
**Designed to:** Strip a long procedure down to the decisions and actions an engineer needs under pressure, without having to read the full document.

---

## 2. Ticket Lifecycle & Quality

### Ticket Creation & Quality SOP 🔗
**Purpose:** Sets the standard for what a complete, usable ticket looks like from creation to closure.
**Designed to:**
- Merge two overlapping legacy documents into one source of truth
- Define required fields, documentation quality, and time-entry expectations
- Reduce rework and back-and-forth caused by incomplete tickets

### Bundled (Parent/Child) Tickets Standard 🆕
**Purpose:** Defines when and how related tickets should be grouped under a parent ticket.
**Designed to:** Prevent duplicate work and fragmented communication when one root issue generates many tickets.

---

## 3. Escalation & Handoffs

### Escalation Procedure 🔄
**Purpose:** Defines when and how a ticket moves from one support tier to the next.
**Designed to:**
- Replace an outdated queue-based model with direct ownership handoffs
- Ensure every ticket always has a named owner
- Define what information must be documented before escalating

### Third-Party Ticket Transfer Procedures 🔗
**Purpose:** Explains how to identify and transfer tickets that belong to partner or specialty teams.
**Designed to:** Expand a narrow single-team procedure into a general transfer process and pair it with the contact information needed to complete the handoff.

### Partner Contact Quick Reference 🆕
**Purpose:** A one-page cheat sheet of the correct channels for each support team and partner.
**Designed to:** Give engineers the right contact at a glance, without the surrounding procedure.

---

## 4. Communication & Notification

### Impact Notification SOP 🆕
**Purpose:** Step-by-step procedure for first-line engineers handling notifications that indicate service impact.
**Designed to:** Make sure impact is assessed consistently and that the right people are informed quickly.

### Impact Notification Workflow 🆕
**Purpose:** A shorter companion workflow to the SOP above.
**Designed to:** Provide a fast visual/decision path for experienced staff, while the full SOP serves as the training reference.

---

## 5. Roles, Responsibilities & Career Development

### Support Tier Roles & Responsibilities 🔄
**Purpose:** Defines the responsibilities of each support tier after an organizational restructure.
**Designed to:** Document the retirement of a legacy tier and clearly reassign its responsibilities, so the written structure matches how the team actually operates.

### Support Engineer Expectations 🔄
**Purpose:** States performance, conduct, and on-call expectations for first- and second-line engineers.
**Designed to:** Bring a multi-year-old document current without changing the standards that still applied.

### Career Progression Matrix 🔄
**Purpose:** Maps each support role to its responsibilities, skills, and recommended certifications.
**Designed to:**
- Show engineers a clear path from one level to the next
- Align certifications with the actual work of each role, not just general prestige
- Flag certifications that are being retired by the vendor, with their replacements

---

## 6. Onboarding & Training

### Help Desk Engineer Training Program 🆕
**Purpose:** A structured multi-week onboarding curriculum for new first-line engineers.
**Designed to:**
- Move new hires from shadowing to independent work in defined stages
- Set weekly objectives and checkpoints
- Reduce time to productivity and make onboarding consistent between trainers
