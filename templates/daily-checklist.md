# Service Coordinator: Daily Shift Checklist

Copy this into a daily note or issue, and check items off as you go.

## Start of shift
- [ ] Review all assigned boards before taking new calls or tickets
- [ ] Close or update stale or incorrectly statused tickets from the prior shift
- [ ] Confirm the new-ticket board is clear
- [ ] Note who is next in the rotation
- [ ] Post overnight items needing attention in the L1 channel
- [ ] Check which technicians are out today, and review and reassign their boards based on need

## Throughout the shift
- [ ] Triage every new ticket (see [ticket triage checklist](ticket-triage-checklist.md))
- [ ] Check the Dispatch Portal for utilization before assigning
- [ ] Schedule appointments by phone, confirming date, time, and scope
- [ ] Log appointments in the ticket and notify the technician
- [ ] Re-triage any ticket with no movement or no clear owner
- [ ] Escalate possible P1s to the Major Incident process right away

## End of shift
- [ ] Follow up on completed appointments, comparing actual time with scheduled time
- [ ] Confirm no tickets are sitting unassigned
- [ ] Hand off anything open to the next shift or after-hours coverage

## Weekly
- [ ] Pull KPIs: response time, resolution time, SLA breaches, technician utilization
- [ ] Review the KPIs with the service manager
